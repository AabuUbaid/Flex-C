FAQ

Is Flex-C VAT compliant for the UAE?

It issues 5% VAT invoices with your TRN and a VAT return summary. FTA e-invoicing through an accredited provider can be added as a custom project.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, the POS keeps billing offline and syncs when the connection returns.

How many users can I add?

Unlimited users per shop on every plan.

Who owns my data?

You do. You can export everything at any time from the Back office.
