Flex-C – One Simple Business Management System

Flex-C is a business management product developed by ADNYS for shops and small businesses across the UAE. It brings everyday business operations together in one system. Manage counter billing, sales, stock, purchases and business records with dedicated tools for owners, cashiers and managers.
